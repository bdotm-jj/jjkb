Retests are consistently faster. The process is efficient — initial rounds catch the bulk of issues, leaving minimal rework for subsequent rounds.
4.6d

Initial testing averages under one work week. This is a healthy baseline — complex projects like Chubb API and Munich RE (10d) are clear outliers, not the norm.

Bottom line
