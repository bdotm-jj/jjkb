Key Findings & Recommendations

  • Avg total duration up modestly (+6%): 8.75d baseline → 9.27d current, driven mainly by two outliers — Workflow Dashboard (25d) and Scottsdale API (21d) — rather than a broad slowdown.
  • R1 durations lengthening (+21%): avg R1 rose from 5.25d to 6.36d. Larger/more complex integrations (Workflow Dashboard R1 = 20d) are pulling the average up.
  • R1→R2 efficiency slightly improved: the proportional drop from R1 to R2 is now 46.1% vs 42.9% at baseline — once a second round starts, issues are resolved faster relative to the first round.
  • Testing volume increased: 11 projects completed at least one round in the current period vs 8 in the baseline window, despite a shorter elapsed window (Apr–Aug vs Nov–Mar).
  • R3 sample is thin but elevated: only 2 current-period projects reached R3 (avg 4.0d) vs baseline avg 1.75d — too small a sample to call a trend, but worth tracking as more projects progress.

Recommendations

  • Review R1 entry criteria for large/complex integrations (e.g., Workflow Dashboard, Scottsdale API) — earlier readiness checks may shorten first-round durations.
  • Since R2 rounds are resolving faster than R1 proportionally, consider applying R2-style triage/kickoff practices earlier, at R1 start.
  • Continue monitoring R3+ rounds next month as sample size grows before drawing conclusions about late-round duration trends.
