Sprint notes
How completion is measured. Each sprint's completion % is taken as published by the LOB and plotted against the goal. Teams report it on a consistent in-our-control basis (completed work ÷ the sprint's ticket commitment, with work blocked outside the team — pending a 3rd party, waiting on the customer, or displaced mid-sprint — handled per the LOB's own method). "Closed" is the LOB's completed-ticket count for the sprint; "Committed" is the tickets committed at sprint start.
