Methodology & notes

Inclusion criteria
Only phases with Status = Complete and an end date within the quarter are counted. In-progress or future phases are excluded from averages.
Duration calculation
Days = End Date − Start Date + 1 (inclusive of both endpoints). Calculated from phase-level header rows in each project plan sheet.
Q1 cutoff
March 30, 2026. Projects where phases ended April 1 or later are excluded from Q1 even if the project was nearly complete.
Data source
Smartsheet Project Plans across all lines of business (Commercial Lines, Personal Lines, Motion, Flood, Support Services). 17 projects included in Q1 baseline.
Outlier note
Alpha Testing and UAT averages are elevated by a small number of long-running projects (GLISE Lifecycle, Chubb Local Rating). Low n counts for Stabilization and Retrospective — interpret with caution.
Update schedule
Update monthly using the Cowork instructions provided. Add Q2 data beginning May 2026, Q3 beginning August 2026, Q4 beginning November 2026.
